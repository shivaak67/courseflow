SMS Terms & Conditions

Prioritize provides optional SMS notifications for your academic tasks, assignments, and calendar events.

Enrollment and messages

By enabling SMS reminders, you agree to receive automated texts from Prioritize about the tasks and deadlines you select, as well as phone verification codes. Enter your own mobile number, select the SMS consent checkbox in Settings, and verify your number to enroll. Consent is optional and is not required to use the app.

Message frequency varies based on the reminders you schedule. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping messages and getting help

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You can also disable SMS reminders in Settings. Delivery depends on your mobile carrier and is not guaranteed; check the app for your deadlines.
