Privacy Policy

Prioritize is an academic task and calendar app. This policy describes how we use information for the app and its optional SMS reminder service.

Information we use

We use your account information and the tasks, assignments, and calendar events you enter to provide your account and schedule. If you choose SMS reminders, we collect your mobile phone number, SMS consent preference, verification status, and the reminder details and times you select.

Optional Canvas calendar connection

If you connect a Canvas calendar feed, we store its private link encrypted and periodically retrieve assignment deadlines and calendar events to display in your account. We do not need your Canvas password or an API access token. Disconnecting in Settings deletes the stored connection and its imported calendar copies from the app.

How SMS information is used

We use your phone number to send verification codes and the task, assignment, and calendar reminders you request. Our messaging provider, Twilio, processes your number and message content to deliver these texts.

Mobile information and consent

We do not sell your personal information. We never share, sell, or provide mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Sharing with Twilio is limited to providing the messaging service; it is not permission to use your mobile information or consent for marketing.

Your choices

SMS is optional and is not required to use Prioritize. Enter your mobile number in Settings, actively select the consent checkbox, and verify your number to enroll. Message frequency varies based on the reminders you schedule. Message and data rates may apply.

You can disable SMS reminders in Settings or reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
